# rr_pre_period

Rerouting device with `--device.rerouting.pre-period 5`: vehicles are rerouted in the 5 s before
departure as well as every 10 s while driving. Parallel-edge net with top edge `11` closed.
Vehicles depart at 10 s intervals so the pre-departure rerouting has a chance to act.
Refs generated with pristine C++ 1.28.0 (WSL).
