# Case `energy_battery_params`: battery device parameters on DEFAULT_VEHTYPE

Energy/unknown vType with vehicle energy attributes (mass, frontSurfaceArea, airDragCoefficient, rollDragCoefficient,
recuperationEfficiency) and battery attributes (maximumBatteryCapacity, actualBatteryCapacity, maximumChargeRate,
stoppingThreshold), `has.battery.device=true`. Battery output: `--battery-output battery --battery-output.precision 4`.
