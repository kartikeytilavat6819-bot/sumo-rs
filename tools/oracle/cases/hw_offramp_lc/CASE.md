# hw_offramp_lc

Three-lane highway (E0 -> E1 -> E2, 25 m/s) with a one-lane off-ramp (off, 15 m/s) diverging
north from n2. Exiting vehicles must move to the rightmost lane of E1 to reach the off-ramp,
creating lane-change pressure against through traffic.

Sim: `--end 200`. Net from node/edge XML via `net_ref.sh`.
