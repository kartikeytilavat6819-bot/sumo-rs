# hw_weaving

Two-lane highway with a short 100 m weaving section: on-ramp (ramp) merges at n1 and an
off-ramp (off) diverges at n2. Vehicles entering from the ramp and leaving via the off-ramp
must cross the through lanes inside the weaving segment E1.

Sim: `--end 200`. Net from node/edge XML via `net_ref.sh`.
