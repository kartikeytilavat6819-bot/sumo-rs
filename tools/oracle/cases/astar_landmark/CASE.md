# A* with ALT landmark distances (plain and gzip tables)

Covers `--astar.landmark-distances` on the sumo side (MSRoutingEngine, `routing-algorithm astar`).
The table is written by pristine `duarouter --astar.save-landmark-distances` and loaded by sumo.

- Net and routes: copies of `reroute_alt` (`ref_simple.net.xml`, `routes.rou`); closure `closing.add.xml`.
- Landmarks: `lm.txt` (edges 10, 12, 13). Tables: `table.txt` (plain) and `table.gz` (zlib gzip, `.gz` name).
- `ref.*` (`sim.args`): plain table. `ref_gz.*` (`sim_gz.args`): gzip table, same expected result.
- All refs generated by `~/sumo-pristine` (sumo and duarouter 1.28.0). Timestamps in the headers differ, nothing else.
